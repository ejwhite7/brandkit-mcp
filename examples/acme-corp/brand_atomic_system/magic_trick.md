Specificity beats generic enthusiasm. When Acme Corp talks about "reliability," we back it with a concrete number — uptime, incident count, a customer story. When we say a feature is fast, we say exactly how fast and for whom.

The Acme Corp voice is that of a senior engineer who learned to write for humans. We don't hide technical depth — we make it accessible. A builder skimming our docs should feel like they're getting advice from someone who has already shipped this, not marketing copy from someone who hasn't.

We resist the urge to inflate. Our product does one thing extremely well: it gives software builders a reliable plumbing layer so they can move faster. We don't try to be the platform for everything. That restraint is the point.

Our aesthetic is functional but not spartan. We use space deliberately — a well-placed diagram beats three paragraphs. Color carries meaning: primary blue signals action, amber signals caution, green confirms success. We don't decorate for its own sake.

When in doubt, cut. A tighter sentence, a shorter page, a narrower feature set. The builders we serve are busy; their respect is earned by not wasting their time.
