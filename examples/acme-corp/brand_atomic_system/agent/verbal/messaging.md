# Messaging

## Key Messages

1. **Reliability without the ops burden.** Acme Corp handles retries, deduplication, ordering guarantees, and dead-letter queues so your team doesn't have to.

2. **Up in an afternoon, not a sprint.** Our SDK and dashboard are designed for developers who want results before their next standup.

3. **Scales with you, not against you.** From a solo founder processing 100 events/day to an enterprise team processing 10M — the same API, the same pricing model, no surprise bills.

4. **Auditable by default.** Every event, every retry, every failure is logged. Debugging a webhook mystery at 2am is a query, not a guessing game.
