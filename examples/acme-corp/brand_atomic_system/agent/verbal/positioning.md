# Positioning

Acme Corp is the infrastructure layer for software builders who need reliable, low-drama plumbing — APIs, webhooks, queues, and event streams that just work at any scale.

## Target Market

Independent developers and small-to-midsize engineering teams building production SaaS products. Typically 1–20 engineers. Ops-averse: they want to ship features, not manage infrastructure.

## Value Proposition

Acme Corp eliminates the "glue code" tax. Instead of spending two sprints wiring up retries, dead-letter queues, and idempotency logic, teams integrate Acme in an afternoon and move on.

## One-Sentence Pitch

Acme Corp gives you the plumbing — so you can build the product.
