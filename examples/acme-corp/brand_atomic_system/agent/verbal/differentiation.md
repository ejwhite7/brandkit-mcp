# Differentiation

## What Makes Acme Corp Different

**1. We do one thing and do it well.**
Most competitors bundle event streaming with observability, feature flags, and a data warehouse. Acme Corp is opinionated about scope: we're the plumbing layer. We integrate cleanly with the tools you already use instead of trying to replace them.

**2. Developer experience is the product.**
Competing offerings are designed for enterprise procurement — complex pricing, lengthy onboarding, sales-assisted setup. Acme Corp is built for the engineer who opens the docs at 10pm and wants something working before midnight.

**3. Transparent reliability model.**
We publish our SLAs, our incident history, and our architecture decisions publicly. Builders make better decisions when they understand what they're building on.

**4. No vendor lock-in by design.**
Our event format follows open standards. Migrating off Acme Corp should be a weekend project, not a quarter-long initiative. We believe confidence in switching creates loyalty in staying.
